## Versioning and compatibility

- Version numbers updated in page metadata, headers, app state and host/guest session validation.
- Existing V3.5 settings are preserved when loading V3.6, including **Solo Showdown or Team Battles**, team names, levels and category selections. Unfinished V3.5 rounds are safely reset to setup because the timer and eligibility rules changed.
- The original **24 Team Battles / 20 Solo Showdown minigames** remain available, subject to the pre-existing and updated level restrictions.
- The V3.4 trivia/question banks and other packs are preserved without changes.

## Testing

- Run `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` on Node.js 22+.
- Includes checks of **all 48 Category Flood 1v1 prompts**, same-category instructions, solo/team preservation, all eight Word Recall proficiency variants in both modes, actual rendered timer/equipment labels, and Clock Duel auto-matchmaking/UI restrictions, alongside RPS rules and no regressions in game counts.
- These are automated code/render checks, **not** a claim that multiple physical phones or network sessions were playtested.

## Interpretations

- “A round only counts when a point is made” is treated as **one decisive Rock-Paper-Scissors throw**. The entire minigame finishes as soon as one side wins three decisive throws; tied throws can be repeated as many times as needed. This interpretation matches “a tie does not count as a round.”
- “Contested Word Recall” includes both the two-player Solo Showdown duel and Team Battles, as explicitly requested.
